Commercial & Industrial Microgrids

Energy Storage

Uninterrupted Backup Ecosystems

Never experience a blackout again. Our advanced battery storage solutions switch on instantly, keeping your critical systems active.

We install premium lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery systems coupled with high-speed hybrid inverters. Unlike standard backup generators, our storage systems operate silently, require zero maintenance, emit no fumes, and switch over in less than 10 milliseconds—preventing computers, servers, and smart home appliances from resetting.

  • Chemistry: Cobalt-free Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) for maximum safety and thermal stability.
  • Switching Speed: < 10ms UPS-grade transfer time, keeping sensitive electronics online.
  • Cycle Life: Rated for over 6,000 cycles at 90% Depth of Discharge (DoD)—over 15 years of daily use.
  • Scalability: Modular design allows you to easily expand capacity as your energy needs grow.
