About izzi

An engineering-led energy enterprise built on transparency, safety, and performance.

Our Vision

Uncompromising Energy Engineering

We started with a simple observation: the solar market is flooded with sales companies, but lacking in engineering execution. We set out to change that.

At izzi installation, we believe that transitioning to solar power is a significant, long-term investment. It should be treated as a serious engineering project, not a commodity transaction. That's why we don't employ aggressive sales representatives. Instead, you'll consult directly with qualified electrical engineers who design systems optimized for performance, safety, and financial return.

Whether we are designing a residential hybrid system or a multi-megawatt commercial microgrid, we approach every project with the same rigorous standards of structural integrity and electrical efficiency.

Engineering First

Every system is designed by a qualified engineer using advanced solar modeling software to simulate shading, cable losses, and peak solar irradiance.

Tier-1 Partnerships

We work exclusively with leading Tier-1 global manufacturers (e.g., Sunsynk, Victron, LONGi, BYD) ensuring your system's components are backed by multi-decade warranties.

Certified Workmanship

Our in-house installation teams are fully certified, insured, and undergo continuous safety and technical training. We do not subcontract our core installation work.
